abstract: Small-scale dynamos (SSDs) amplify magnetic fields in turbulent plasmas. Theory predicts nonlinear magnetic energy growth Emag∝tpnl, but this scaling has not been tested across flow regimes. Using a large ensemble of SSD simulations spanning sub- to supersonic turbulence, we find linear growth (pnl=1) in subsonic flows and quadratic growth (pnl=2) in supersonic flows. In all cases, we find a dynamo efficiency of ∼1/100 and a duration Δt≈20t0, with t0 the turnover time, establishing the universal timescales and efficiencies of the nonlinear SSD across astrophysical and laboratory plasmas.
