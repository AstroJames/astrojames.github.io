Congratulations to collaborator and **CITA graduate student Michael Grehan** on his nice new work, *Sparking Dynamic, Relativistic Magnetic Reconnection in a Twisted Flux Tube*, with Christopher Thompson, Bart Ripperda, and me!

The study explores how a localized disturbance sparks reconnection in a twisted flux tube, releasing magnetic energy into plasma motions and heating. The [preprint is now on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22653) and has been submitted to *The Astrophysical Journal*.
