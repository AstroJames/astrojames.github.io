Our paper, *Local relaxation and scale-dependent alignment in compressible, magnetized turbulence*, with Amitava Bhattacharjee has been **accepted in Physical Review Letters**!

The updated study combines simulations with and without a mean magnetic field and a new model for departures from locally relaxed states. Together, they explain how alignment changes with scale in turbulent plasma.
