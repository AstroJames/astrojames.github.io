Congratulations to my collaborator **Mandy Chen at Caltech** on the submission of our paper, *From IFS Maps to 3D Structure I: Constraining Geometry in the Circumgalactic Medium*!

How do we interpret turbulence when we observe only sparse, glowing blobs of plasma? We develop analytical relations, tested against numerical simulations, that connect turbulent velocity scaling to the motions measured in projected emission-line maps. By accounting for the depth of the emitting gas, discrete plasma clumps, and observational resolution, we can use these measurements to constrain the three-dimensional geometry of the gas surrounding galaxies.

The paper is now available on arXiv and has been **submitted to AAS Journals**.

[Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20929).
