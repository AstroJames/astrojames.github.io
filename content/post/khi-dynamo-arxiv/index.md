Our new preprint, *The kinematic Kelvin–Helmholtz instability-driven dynamo: implications for merging compact objects*, is [now on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05880).

When neutron stars merge, their shear layer rolls into Kelvin–Helmholtz billows. We use high-resolution simulations and analytical models to trace how these motions amplify magnetic fields. At high Reynolds number, the models predict magnetar-strength fields on roughly **0.1 ms timescales**, suggesting that global merger simulations may miss this rapid stage.
