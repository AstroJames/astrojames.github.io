Probability density functions (PDFs)

What the surface and arrows represent

The original preset follows the 95th percentile of the baroclinic-source magnitude, smoothed over 0.8 voxels. Full-volume mode extracts a new isosurface at the chosen field value across the whole 128³ cutout, including material outside the remnant mask. The level is divided by the field’s interior mean or RMS. Smoothing changes only the shape field. Geometry stays fixed when the color field changes. These diagnostic isosurfaces are not fitted shock fronts. Classical marching cubes can resolve ambiguous cells differently from the original preset.

Density, pressure, and the temperature proxy P/ρ are divided by their mean within the remnant mask. Vorticity and baroclinic-source magnitudes are divided by their RMS within that mask. These positive fields use logarithmic colors, limited to their surface 2nd–98th percentiles. Values outside the scale saturate at the end colors. Physical-unit conversions are not assumed.

Here b = ∇ρ × ∇P / ρ² is the physical baroclinic source. The signed quantity ω · b shows the baroclinic contribution to the evolution of |ω|²/2. Positive values reinforce existing vorticity, while negative values oppose it. It is one contribution to the enstrophy budget. The alignment view separates direction from magnitude.

Arrows sample the selected vector at well-spaced locations on the current surface. Full-volume mode uses farthest-point sampling from up to 4,000 candidate vertices. Equal-length arrows show direction. With magnitude scaling enabled, lengths are proportional to magnitude up to the 95th percentile of up to 800 sampled vectors. Velocity is shown in the simulation frame, without subtracting remnant motion. Vorticity arrows mark rotation axes, not flow trajectories. Increasing transparency reveals arrows behind the surface but blends the surface colors.

PDFs span the full finite range of the selected quantity, including values beyond the color limits. Surface PDFs update with the geometry and approximate area weighting by assigning one third of each triangle’s area to each vertex. Interior PDFs use equal-volume cells in the remnant mask. Each PDF integrates to one over its included samples. Logarithmic quantities have a density per unit log₁₀. Vector PDFs use the magnitude of the interpolated vector and its interior RMS. Cutaways, camera views, and arrow counts do not filter these distributions.

Full-volume data retain the original float32 values with lossless compression. Temperature is reconstructed as P/ρ and magnitudes from the saved vector components. Colors and vectors use trilinear interpolation on the original grid. Fast geometry samples the smoothed shape field on a 64³ grid spanning the same cutout. Surface PDFs describe that displayed mesh. The slice is sampled at 128² points, and its contour lines use linear interpolation within triangles. Undefined color values appear gray.
